name: Comic requirements
description: The user's essential expectations for this Kazakh traditions comic app.
---

The user needs actual illustrated, cartoon-style comics, not prompts or text scenarios alone. Stories should show a little girl asking a grandmother or grandfather about Kazakh traditions, with a Kazakh setting such as a киіз үй.

**Why:** The user repeated that script-only output was not the requested product and gave the example of a girl asking her grandmother about a baby in a бесік.

**How to apply:** Every successful comic-generation result must contain illustrations and dialogue. Do not silently replace a failed illustration with a text-only result.

Keep the overall presentation compact. Do not show a sample three-panel comic or reflection questions in the headline/hero area.

**Why:** After reviewing the illustrated hero, the user explicitly asked to remove its three pictures and questions and replace them with something else.

**How to apply:** Reserve illustrated panels and reflection questions for generated results. Keep the introduction simple, without oversized typography or spacing.

Keep three illustrated panels in one horizontal row on all screen sizes, with dialogue directly on the pictures. Below every comic, include “Дәстүрдің мәні” and a family reflection assignment: ask parents about the tradition, describe what happened (or why it was not held), and explain the child's understanding.

**Why:** The user confirmed they like the three-panel approach, explicitly requested horizontal rather than vertical placement, and asked for these learning prompts, using тұсаукесер as an example.

**How to apply:** Use horizontal scrolling on narrow screens rather than stacking panels. Match the question to the comic's tradition rather than asking about тұсаукесер for every story. Do not restore the removed introductory sentence describing Айша learning through a three-panel comic.

The requested theme/headline is “Қазақ халқының дәстүрлерін комикс арқылы насихаттау”.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this wording.

**How to apply:** Preserve this theme when changing the app's copy.
